## Description (4,000 char max)

**Life, on the hour.**

Ralli is a new kind of social app built for the people you actually care about — not an audience, not strangers, just your close friends. Every hour, everyone posts a quick log: where you are, what you're doing, how the day's going. No filters to perform for, no algorithm deciding who sees you. Just your people, showing up for each other, hour by hour.

**See your friends' hours, not their highlight reel**
Open Ralli and see what your crew is actually up to right now — not a curated version from three days ago. A quick video, a photo, a moment. It takes seconds to post and seconds to catch up on everyone.

**Turn "what's everyone doing" into "let's go"**
Ralli doesn't stop at knowing what your friends are up to — it helps you actually do something about it. Discover real spots nearby with honest, crowd-sourced insight on when to go and what to expect. Drop a Beacon to let friends know you're heading somewhere and see who wants in. Presence turns into plans.

**Real conversations, not just reactions**
Message your friends directly, react to their logs, keep your streaks alive. Ralli's chat is built for actually talking, not just double-tapping.

**Built for real friend groups**
Whether it's your roommates, your team, or your whole friend group, Ralli keeps everyone in sync without the noise of a broadcast feed. Add friends by handle, QR code, or a shareable code — no public follower counts, no strangers in your feed.

**What you can do in Ralli:**
— Post a video or photo log each hour to keep your friends in the loop
— Scroll a feed built only from people you actually know
— Discover nearby spots with real, useful context before you go
— Start or join a Beacon to turn plans into an actual hangout
— Chat in real time, react, and keep your streaks going
— Build your friend circle with handles, QR codes, and invite codes

Ralli is currently in beta and growing one friend group at a time. We're building this with our earliest users, so if something's missing, tell us — we're listening and shipping fast.

Life's happening by the hour. Come Ralli.
